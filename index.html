How It Works

1

Create Your Items

Add raw materials and components with quantity, pricing, and images. These are the building blocks of your products.

2

Connect to Products

Link Shopify products to items they're made from. Example: "Handmade Soap" uses 2× Essential Oil + 1× Soap Base.

3

Automatic Updates

When a product sells, the app automatically deducts raw materials. When you restock, products update in Shopify.

4

Track & Analyze

View your dashboard for insights into inventory changes, sales impact, and reorder points.
