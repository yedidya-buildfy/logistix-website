Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 6, 2025

Introduction

Logistix ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our Shopify app for inventory management.

Information We Collect

When you install and use Logistix, we access and process the following data from your Shopify store:

Store Data

  • Products: Product names, descriptions, variants, SKUs, and pricing to enable inventory tracking
  • Inventory: Inventory levels and stock quantities to synchronize with raw material items
  • Orders: Order information including line items and quantities to automatically adjust raw material inventory
  • Images: Product and item images uploaded through our app, stored on Shopify's CDN

Account Information

  • Your Shopify store domain and shop ID
  • Store owner email address for account management and notifications
  • App installation and authentication tokens

Usage Data

  • Item creation, updates, and deletion actions
  • Product-to-item connection records
  • Inventory change logs and synchronization history
  • App usage analytics and feature interactions

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve Logistix services:

  • Inventory Management: Track raw materials, connect them to products, and automatically adjust quantities based on orders
  • Real-Time Synchronization: Keep inventory levels synchronized between your items and Shopify products
  • Analytics & Insights: Provide dashboard metrics, reorder alerts, and usage trends
  • Customer Support: Troubleshoot issues and respond to your inquiries
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and performance
  • Service Communication: Send important updates about the app, billing, and features

Data Storage and Security

Where We Store Data

  • All data is stored securely in encrypted PostgreSQL databases
  • Images are stored on Shopify's secure CDN infrastructure
  • Database backups are encrypted and stored securely
  • Our infrastructure is hosted on secure cloud providers with industry-standard security practices

Security Measures

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Database connections are encrypted and use secure authentication
  • Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We implement regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Authentication tokens are securely stored and never exposed

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • Shopify: We share data with Shopify as required to provide app functionality through their API
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting providers (database, storage) who operate under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights

We never use your data for marketing purposes or share it with advertising platforms.

Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we store about your store
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data by uninstalling the app
  • Export: Export your items, products, and inventory data at any time through the app
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format (CSV)

To exercise these rights, contact us at office@falcon-cro.com

Data Retention and Deletion

Active Users

We retain your data for as long as you have Logistix installed and actively use it to provide continuous service.

Upon Uninstallation

When you uninstall Logistix:

  • Your store data (products, orders, inventory) stops being accessed immediately
  • App-specific data (items, connections, logs) is retained for 30 days to allow reinstallation
  • After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted from our systems
  • Images stored on Shopify's CDN are deleted within 7 days

Immediate Deletion

To request immediate deletion of all data, email us at office@falcon-cro.com with your store domain.

Cookies and Tracking

Logistix uses minimal cookies and tracking:

  • Authentication Cookies: To maintain your logged-in session (essential)
  • Shopify Session Tokens: Required for app functionality within Shopify admin
  • Analytics: We collect anonymous usage data to improve the app (no personal identifiers)

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

Compliance

Logistix complies with:

  • GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation (European Union)
  • CCPA: California Consumer Privacy Act
  • Shopify's API Terms: All Shopify app requirements and data handling policies
  • Data Protection Laws: Applicable data protection regulations in your jurisdiction

Children's Privacy

Logistix is designed for businesses and not intended for use by individuals under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make changes:

  • The "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised
  • Significant changes will be notified via email to your store owner address
  • Continued use of Logistix after changes constitutes acceptance

Contact Us

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data:

Email: office@falcon-cro.com

App Support: Use the live chat widget in the app

We will respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 48 hours.
